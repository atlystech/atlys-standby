FAQ

Good questions.

Is my data safe during the cleanse?+

Yes - a full backup is taken before any changes are made, and nothing still in active use is touched. Older records are compressed, not deleted.

Does this only work with MYOB Acumatica?+

Atlys Cleanse was built in response to MYOB Acumatica's new storage charges, and works there - it also applies to Acumatica and adjacent platforms carrying the same kind of database bloat.

How is it priced?+

A fixed one-off fee based on your system size and configuration, quoted after a quick scoping conversation. Early-request pricing is available now - contact us before the standard rate applies.

Can I run it more than once?+

Yes - there's no subscription tying you to a schedule. Many teams run it annually as part of general ERP upkeep.

How much storage and cost will I actually save?+

It depends on your system. Storage and cost reductions vary by system configuration - how much duplicate data has accumulated, how long the system has been running, and how your modules and attachments are set up - so results are not guaranteed. The initial scan tells you what's recoverable in your database before you commit.
